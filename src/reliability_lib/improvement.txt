Reliability review
==================

Four-mode status: one-shot PASS; long-term PASS through the shared runner; monitoring PASS through the shared runner; event-based PASS through the shared runner. Current automated execution proves only one-shot.

Gaps and incompatibilities
- Burn-in, thermal cycling, power cycling, MTBF, and aging concepts are not equivalent to rerunning a full test suite at a polling interval.
- Repeating stress or power actions can damage hardware, reset the target, or invalidate accumulated measurements.
- Reliability measurements lack run identity, elapsed exposure, sample count, and persistence across process restarts.
- Historical USB/error events can be stale and repeatedly reported.

Improvements
- Model reliability campaigns as explicit long-term jobs with setup, teardown, checkpoints, and durable results.
- Restrict monitoring/event modes to passive health/error counters; require explicit opt-in for stress and power cycling.
- Add injected fault/event sources and deterministic fixtures for native CI.
- Add bounded four-mode tests for campaign timeout, pause/resume, interruption cleanup, and event de-duplication.
